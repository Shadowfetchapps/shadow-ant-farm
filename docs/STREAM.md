**Shadow Ant Farm: a live glass ant farm, digging around the clock**

A few hundred worker ants are building their nest between two panes of glass, live. Nothing is scripted and
nothing repeats. Every tunnel you see was dug by individual ants deciding, one grain at a time, where to dig next.

**What you're watching**

- Digging happens only at exposed soil faces. Each worker carries its pellet up and out and drops it on the
  spoil mounds, which slowly grow and slump on the surface.
- Galleries branch, deepen and widen into chambers over the day. A new colony starts from untouched soil with
  no tunnels at all.
- Foragers carry seeds from the feeding stone down into the nest. The keeper tops the stone up every few hours.
- Ants rest, groom and wander between shifts. All the sound is the colony itself: digging, crumbling soil and
  footsteps on the glass.

**Good to know**

- It's a computer simulation, rendered in real time, of what an ant farm looks like. It's tuned to look
  believable over a whole day, not to model a particular species precisely.
- Each colony comes from a random seed. Today's seed is shown in the operator notes if you want to compare.
- No real animals are involved.
